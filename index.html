Structural Drying

Water removal and structural drying are closely connected, but they are not the same thing.

Extraction removes excess water.

Structural drying addresses moisture that remains inside affected building materials.

Professional drying may use:

Air Movers

Air movers increase airflow around wet materials.

Dehumidifiers

Dehumidification helps remove excess moisture from the surrounding air.

Moisture Detection

Moisture readings can help identify wet areas and monitor drying progress.

Materials may look dry on the outside while still containing moisture internally.

That is why moisture monitoring can be an important part of professional restoration.
